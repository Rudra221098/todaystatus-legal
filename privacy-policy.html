Privacy Policy

TodayStatus · Developed by Rudra Sukhadiya · Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TodayStatus, developed by Rudra Sukhadiya ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app. Last updated: 11 October 2026.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and profile photo, which you provide when you sign up (including via Google Sign-In) or edit in your profile, along with a unique user ID that we assign to your account. Passwords are handled securely by our authentication provider and are never stored in plain text.
  • Device information: device model, OS version, and a device token used to deliver notifications (e.g. daily "Good Morning" / "Good Night" templates and festival reminders).
  • Usage data: templates you view, download, or share, and general app interaction, used to improve content and performance.
  • Purchase information: if you buy TodayStatus Premium, purchase details are processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your card or payment details.
  • Custom design requests: if you use our custom banner / poster service, any text, photos, or details you share with us through chat are used solely to create your design and are not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Personalised videos: when you download or share a video template, our server combines your profile photo (and your name, if you are not a Premium member) with the template to create a personalised video. The finished video is stored in our cloud storage and linked to your account.
  • Content you view and share: the news city/state you select, the horoscope sign (rashi) you view, and the templates, remedies, or horoscopes you share or save, used to show you relevant daily content.
  • Sharing & saving: when you share or save content (to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or your gallery), the content is handled by the app or service you choose. We do not receive your contacts or your messages on those apps.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver daily status templates and notifications.
  • To create the personalised videos and custom designs you request.
  • To manage your Premium membership and unlock paid features.
  • To personalize content such as language and festival packs.
  • To respond to support requests sent via Contact Us.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse.

Daily News, Horoscope & Home Remedies

TodayStatus delivers fresh content every day, including local and national news, daily horoscopes, and traditional home remedies.

  • News: we use the city or state you choose to show you relevant news and videos. We do not need your precise GPS location for this. News and videos come from third-party sources, and we are not responsible for their content.
  • Horoscope: the sign you select is used only to show your daily horoscope, Panchang, and related videos. Horoscopes are general, for information and entertainment only, and are not professional astrological advice.
  • Home remedies: remedies are traditional, general information and not medical advice. Please consult a doctor for any health problem.
  • Videos: news and horoscope videos may be played through YouTube. YouTube may collect data as per its own privacy policy when you watch them.

Sharing Content

You can share or save templates, news, horoscopes, and remedies as images, videos, or text. When you share, the content (including your name and photo if shown on a template) is sent to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook, and is then governed by that app's privacy policy. Saving to your gallery needs your permission, which you can turn off anytime in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

TodayStatus uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase – provides our backend infrastructure, including account authentication, database, and real-time chat for custom design requests.
  • Cloudflare R2 – used to store and deliver images, videos, and files, including what you upload in the app (such as your profile photo, reference photos, and custom design chat files) and the personalised videos created when you download or share a video template.
  • Render – hosts the server that creates personalised videos. It temporarily handles your photo and the template video while the video is being made.
  • Google Play Billing – processes Premium plan purchases.
  • RevenueCat – helps manage and verify Premium purchases made through Google Play.
  • Firebase – used to deliver push notifications and to support Google Sign-In.
  • Google Sign-In – lets you log in with your Google account.
  • YouTube – used to play news and horoscope videos inside the app, governed by Google's privacy policy.

Advertising & Personalization

TodayStatus does not currently show third-party ads. If we introduce advertising in the future (for example, through Google AdMob), we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly and request any consent required by your region's law before personalized ads are shown.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers (such as Supabase, Cloudflare, Render, RevenueCat, and Google) as needed to operate the app, or when required by law.

Data Retention & Security

We keep your account information, chat messages, custom design requests, uploaded photos, and the personalised videos created for you until you delete your account (or ask us to), after which we delete them. We may keep limited records where the law requires it. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypting it in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

TodayStatus is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your Choices

  • You can disable notifications from your device settings.
  • You can edit your name and profile photo anytime from Edit Profile in the app.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete Account. You can also delete it from our Delete Account page, or request deletion by emailing todaystatusofficial@gmail.com, and we will process it within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
