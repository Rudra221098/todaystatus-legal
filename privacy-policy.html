Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TodayStatus ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app. Last updated: 17 August 2026.

Information We Collect

  • Device information: device model, OS version, and a device token used to deliver notifications (e.g. daily "Good Morning" / "Good Night" templates and festival reminders).
  • Usage data: screens viewed, templates downloaded, and general app interaction, used to improve content and performance.
  • Purchase information: if you subscribe to TodayStatus Premium, purchase details are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your card or payment details.
  • Custom design requests: if you use our custom banner / poster service, any text, photos, or details you share with us through chat are used solely to create your design and are not sold or shared with third parties.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver daily status templates and notifications.
  • To manage your Premium membership and unlock paid features.
  • To personalize content such as language and festival packs.
  • To respond to support requests sent via Contact Us.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse.

Third-Party Services

TodayStatus uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase – provides our backend infrastructure, including account authentication, database, and real-time chat for custom design requests.
  • Cloudflare R2 – used to store and deliver images and files you upload in the app (such as your profile photo, reference photos, and custom design chat files).
  • Google Play Billing – processes Premium subscription and lifetime plan purchases.

Advertising & Personalization

TodayStatus does not currently show third-party ads. If we introduce advertising in the future (for example, through Google AdMob), we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly and request any consent required by your region's law before personalized ads are shown.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers (such as Google, for ads, billing, and notifications) as needed to operate the app, or when required by law.

Data Retention & Security

We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes above, and take reasonable technical measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

TodayStatus is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your Choices

  • You can disable notifications from your device settings.
  • You can opt out of ad personalization from your Google Account settings.
  • You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
