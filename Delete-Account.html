Delete Your Account

TodayStatus · Last updated: 11 October 2026

You can delete your TodayStatus account and the data associated with it at any time. Choose either option below.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the TodayStatus app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us at todaystatusofficial@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my TodayStatus account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details: name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Your custom design requests and chat messages, and the photos and files you uploaded.
  • The personalised videos created from your photo, and the related processing records.
  • Your sign-in (login) account.
  • Your saved preferences, such as selected city, state, and language.

What we may keep

  • Purchase records held by Google Play, which are managed by Google under its own policies.
  • Limited information we are required to keep by law, for example for tax or fraud-prevention purposes.

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. An active Premium purchase is not refunded because of account deletion.

Active subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an active Monthly or Yearly subscription. Please cancel it first in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions, otherwise Google Play may continue to charge you.

How long it takes

When you delete your account from inside the app, we start deleting your data right away. When you request deletion by email, we verify the request and complete it within 30 days. Data is not kept beyond this, except for the items listed under "What we may keep".
